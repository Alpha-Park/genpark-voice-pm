# GenPark Voice PM

Why pay for a "Say it and it's done" audio-productivity app? This skill replaces premium voice note apps by automatically transcribing your audio, identifying tasks, and generating structured to-do lists.

## Usage
`openclaw use genpark-voice-pm --audio "brainstorming.m4a"`

The agent will process the audio, summarize the context, extract bulleted action items, and format them into markdown or push them directly to your calendar/todo app via OpenClaw integrations.
